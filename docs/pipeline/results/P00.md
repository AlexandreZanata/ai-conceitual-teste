## What we learned
The RTX 4060 Mobile sustains ~24.6 TFLOP/s bf16 matmul for a full 10 minutes with almost no thermal drop from peak (~24.3). At MFU 0.25 the 42.2M × 4B-token run is feasible in ~45.8 h, leaving ~26 h of the 72 h cap. Usable VRAM is 7,174 MB after a 5% back-off from OOM — enough for the planned design with activation checkpointing, but tighter than the datasheet 8,188 MB.

## What surprised us
Sustained ≈ peak (prediction expected a 15–25 vs 25–35 split). HBM copy bandwidth measured 112 GB/s vs a 180 GB/s guess. Disk sequential read hit 4.5 GB/s. Peak sat at the floor of the 25–35 TFLOP/s band.
