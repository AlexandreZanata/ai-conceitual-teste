## What we learned
FineWeb-Edu sample-10BT alone, after exact dedup and a provisional 16,384 BPE, yields **4.42 B** tokens at **4.30 bytes/token**. Stack/Cosmopedia/Wikipedia/arXiv were deferred (Stack HF-gated); FineWeb was widened to hit the gate. Upstream FineWeb MinHash made a full near-LSH pass a no-op (probe 0.01%); residual on 100k was 0.018%. Held-out is document-level (8,408 docs). BPB remains null until a model exists.

## What surprised us
Exact-dup rate on cleaned FineWeb was only **1.88%** removed (prediction implied heavier web duplication). Near-dup residual was **45× below** the 2% cap without a full LSH rewrite. Token yield at vocab 16k beat the 3.5 bytes/token planning figure (measured 4.30) — fewer tokens per byte, still cleared 4.0 B from 19 GB unique text.
