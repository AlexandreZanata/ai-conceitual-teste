\title{Nano Generative Students under a Hard Parameter Cap:\\
Honest Product Gains, Anti-False-Positive Evaluation,\\
and Deferred Open-Ended Generation}
\author{Alexandre Zanata}
\date{\today}

\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
We report an archival study of a nano causal language-model student under a hard
parameter budget ($\leq$5M) trained and served with curated public programming and
frontier corpora. Across research waves W--BA we separate three layers that are often
conflated: (i)~efficiency tips and recipes (train/serve latency and long-context
packaging), (ii)~a labeled interactive product stack (known-ask lookup, semantic wrap,
peak extractive spans, abstain), and (iii)~true novel generative continue.
Using production-path live ask as the evaluation oracle, we show that tip-stack
serve recipes and a labeled known-ask stack yield real, measurable gains in paraphrase
hit-rate, adversary/intent false-hit control, usable context content, and wall-clock
p50/p99 --- while open DECODE without wrap remains unsafe or empty, and
\texttt{true\_continue} stays unmet. We therefore promote a strict ship claim
(AF packaged stack + AQ/AS trust + ablated DECODE) and honestly \textbf{HOLD/DEFER}
nano generative / mini-AGI language until a real new method clears true-continue
under anti-false-positive gates. Negative results (killed tips, rename theater,
pack-only metrics) are first-class evidence.
\end{abstract}
