\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

\subsection{Student, teachers, and data}
The student is a nano causal LM under a hard $\leq$5M parameter budget.
Teachers include TinyStories-scale story models~\cite{tinystories} and a small
code teacher (\texttt{bigcode/tiny\_starcoder\_py})~\cite{tinystarcoder}.
Training and evaluation use curated public corpora (programming tutorials,
Rust book slices, BIP texts, efficiency notes) documented in
\texttt{nano\_lm/data/CURATED-SOURCES.md}.

\subsection{Tip stack and recipes}
Official tips (survivors after kill discipline):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{H-STAG$'$} --- training tip (TIPD).
  \item \textbf{H-EARLY} --- decode speed.
  \item \textbf{H-POOL} --- quality at wall.
\end{itemize}
Deploy recipes (frozen DEPL-Y family) include serve-fast (\textbf{H-PACK}+\textbf{H-QT}),
code-smart (\textbf{H-ABS-QPFB2} with BEAMKV/TCACHE/SCORERAM), and long-context
(\textbf{ROLL}/\textbf{SUMCACHE}/\textbf{GPFB4-LONG}).
Killed lines (STREAM, KVCACHE-Q, GENCACHE, MIXD, GPFB~$K{=}2$, naive CTX) remain
archived and must not be soft-revived.

\subsection{Labeled product stack (Caminho A)}
Interactive serving uses explicit product modes:
\begin{center}
\texttt{LOOKUP} $\mid$ \texttt{PEAK} $\mid$ \texttt{DECODE} $\mid$ \texttt{ABSTAIN}
\end{center}
Known-ask HITL uses \textbf{H-ZWRAP} / \textbf{H-WRAPBANK} / \textbf{H-SEMWRAP}
(\texttt{--wrap --semwrap} on the production ask runner).
UI and logs must print the mode; unlabeled answers are forbidden.
DECODE without content bars may abstain (honest refuse), including after
snippet-prefix / gibberish-tail ablations (STRICT ship law).

\subsection{Anti-false-positive evaluation}
Every promotion stage must publish:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Forever held-out intent false-hit rate (hard 0).
  \item Regression holds on prior held-out packs (e.g.\ AZ div/sub/BIP wrong-slot).
  \item Over-refuse misses on exact gold (hard 0).
  \item Live ask battery on the production path (read completion text; do not trust
        \texttt{ok:true} alone).
  \item Latency p50/p99 per mode on production ask.
  \item Context content bars (howto/cite/long); $L_{\mathrm{eff}}$ alone is insufficient.
  \item Generative true-continue only if novel readable continue (span-fallback $\neq$ gen).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Anti-FP dictionary.}
Intent-mismatch LOOKUP (e.g.\ \texttt{pow}$\rightarrow$\texttt{add},
\texttt{sort}$\rightarrow$\texttt{reverse}) is a \textbf{false-hit}.
Exact-gold ABSTAIN is a \textbf{product miss}.
Pack FH~$0$ with forever FP~$>0$ is \textbf{pack theater}.
SAFE/adversary scores measure wrong-gold refusal, not answer quality.

\subsection{Generative / mini-AGI gate}
Mini-AGI-\emph{inspired} language (retrieve·generate·route·refuse·evaluate) is
allowed only after \texttt{true\_continue} PROMOTE under a \emph{real} method
(distill, hybrid rejector, or named CAPCHECK) --- never via NANOGEN$^*$ rename.
Absent a method, the lab records HOLD/DEFER and keeps the ship claim STRICT.
