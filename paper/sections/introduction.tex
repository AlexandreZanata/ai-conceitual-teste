\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Small language models are frequently marketed with large-model metaphors
(``assistant'', ``AGI-lite'', ``chat'') while evaluation quietly rewards
retrieval hits, cache hits, or in-bank paraphrase matches.
This paper archives a multi-wave laboratory study of a \textbf{nano generative
student} under a hard cap of $\leq$5M parameters, with the explicit north star of
a \emph{mini-AGI-inspired} capability shape --- retrieve, generate, route, refuse,
evaluate --- implemented by a small model, not by an unlabeled frontier chat clone.

\paragraph{Problem.}
Efficiency metrics (wall latency, effective context length) and product wrappers
(known-answer banks) can look like intelligence gains.
Without an anti-false-positive (anti-FP) scoreboard on the \emph{production} ask
path, labs promote LOOKUP scores as IQ, PEAK spans as open chat, and span-fallback
decode as generative unlock.

\paragraph{Contribution.}
We provide:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item A \textbf{three-layer claim discipline}: tip/recipe efficiency; labeled
        product modes (\texttt{LOOKUP}/\texttt{PEAK}/\texttt{DECODE}/\texttt{ABSTAIN});
        true novel continue (generative).
  \item An \textbf{anti-FP evaluation law}: production ask $=$ eval; forever
        held-out intent probes; intent-mismatch LOOKUP $=$ false-hit; exact-gold
        abstain $=$ product miss; pack PASS with forever FP $=$ pack theater.
  \item An archival consolidation of Waves W--BA: promoted recipes, product
        trust path, measurable context/speed boards, and honest generative
        HOLD/DEFER (NANOGEN6--11).
  \item A documented \textbf{negative-results} catalog (killed tips, failed
        interactive decode, rename theater) as scientific evidence.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Headline claim (locked).}
Under $\leq$5M, the lab ships a \textbf{labeled known-ask assistant} with refuse
and mode honesty --- not an unlabeled open-chat LM and not true-continue unlock.
The objective remains a nano generative / mini-AGI-inspired student; the archive
records what is currently \emph{measured}, not what is hoped.
